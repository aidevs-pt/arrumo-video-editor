# Titles, logos and type in motion

- Set the type well before it moves: tight line spacing on headlines, slightly tighter letter spacing on large type, a clear contrast between weights, and line breaks between phrases rather than inside them.
- Match the size of each revealed piece to the tone: whole lines for calm and premium pieces, word by word for energy and hooks, letter by letter only for short words.
- Reveal text by raising it from behind its baseline, wiping it in the reading direction or bringing it into focus. Stagger lines by about a tenth of a second and words by about a twentieth, and finish a whole reveal within a second.
- Type set to a voice or a song: each word or phrase lands as it is said, important words are larger or in the accent, and scale and position change between phrases while the typeface stays the same.
- Logos: one technique per logo (drawing on, building piece by piece, a wipe or a morph), finished within one to two and a half seconds. Keep the mark's proportions, angle and colours exactly as designed, and keep its clear space empty. The name lands last, and with a sound logo the mark settles exactly on its accent. The final frame must be the clean, true logo.
- Intros: one to three seconds with the title and at most a tagline. Never hold back the content behind a long opening.
- End cards: the logo or name and one call to action, held for at least two seconds and calm enough to sit under a platform's end screen.
- Lower thirds: name and role, arriving and leaving within half a second and staying long enough to be read twice.
- Chapter title cards share one design; only the words change.

Checks: the type is readable at phone size; nothing is distorted; each reveal finishes before the line has to be read; the final frame holds still.
