# Platforms and formats

Each format in the brief is a size this video is delivered in. The platform, when the creator named one, is where it will be posted.

- Frame for every format. In a vertical (9:16) format, keep faces, text and key visuals in the upper and middle part of the frame.
- On vertical feeds (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels) the app draws the account name, caption and buttons over the bottom of the video and down its right edge. Keep text and anything important out of the bottom quarter and away from the right edge.
- Feeds decide in the first seconds. Open on the promise, the result or the most striking moment, not on a logo, greeting or slow build-up.
- Many people watch feeds with the sound off. Let on-screen text carry the message, and keep spoken points visible on screen.
- YouTube Shorts can be up to three minutes long. Instagram shows Reels longer than three minutes to followers but not to new audiences, so keep a Reel meant to reach new people under three minutes.
- On YouTube in landscape (16:9), viewers chose to watch: build the argument, mark chapters with clear title cards, and leave room to breathe. If the creator uses end screens, keep the last 5 to 20 seconds visually calm so the end-screen elements do not cover anything important.
- Square and landscape formats in feeds such as LinkedIn often play muted and small: large, high-contrast text and a clear first frame matter most.
