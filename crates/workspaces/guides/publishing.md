# Writing publishing text and thumbnails

- Write as the creator, in the first person, in the language of the creator's context, unless the profile says otherwise.
- Titles promise one specific thing the video delivers. Front-load the important words; keep them short enough not to be cut off on a phone.
- Make A/B variants genuinely different: a different angle, benefit or curiosity gap, not the same title reworded.
- Descriptions open with what the viewer gets, then the details, links and chapters. Never claim what the video does not show.
- Tags and keywords name the topic, products and audience; no unrelated popular terms.
- A thumbnail shows one subject large, with at most a few words, high contrast and the brand's colours. It must still read at a small size.
- Follow the format's own specifications and reference images over these general notes.
