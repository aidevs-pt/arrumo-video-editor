# Storyboarding a video without footage

- Each scene carries one beat of the script. If a beat needs two ideas, make two scenes.
- A viewer with the sound off should still follow the message from the screen.
- Scene length follows reading time: at least 3 seconds, usually 5–7 for a simple card and longer for diagrams. With narration, a scene lasts at least as long as its line plus about a second.
- Land each scene's key visual on the narration's key word, and hold the last frame for a moment before the next scene.
- Keep one visual system across scenes: the same palette, type, margins and motion style. Vary layout, not identity.
- Open with the strongest promise or result. End on a clear call to action or summary card.
- Narration is the script's spoken words, written to be said aloud: short sentences, no lists of numbers, no parentheses.
