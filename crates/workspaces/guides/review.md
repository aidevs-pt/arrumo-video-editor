# Reviewing an edit before showing it

Check your own work as a critic would before the creator sees it. Be specific: a timecode, what is wrong and the fix.

- Watch the joins: no jump in the picture or a word cut short, no click in the sound, no reaction cut before it lands.
- Watch the opening seconds and the ending: does it hook, and does it close cleanly?
- Check that overlays and animations show the right frames at the right moment and that subtitles are never hidden behind them.
- Check readability on a phone: text size, contrast and time on screen.
- Measure rather than assume. Loudness, peaks and lengths are numbers; say what you measured and what you could not check.
- Compare against the creator's notes and learned feedback, and confirm each one was addressed.
- Rank problems by how much they hurt the viewer, and fix the top ones first. If problems remain after a few passes, report them instead of looping.
