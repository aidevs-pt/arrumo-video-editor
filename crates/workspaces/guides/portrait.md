# Choosing portrait cuts

- Each cut stands alone: someone who never saw the full video must understand it. Start on the hook, not on context.
- The first second must show or say something that makes people stay: a result, a surprise, a bold claim the clip then proves, a question it answers, or a line that speaks to exactly who it is for.
- Make cuts of clearly different lengths and angles so the creator can learn which performs best.
- Inside a cut, something should change every few seconds: a new point, a reaction, a demonstration.
- End on a payoff or a natural close, never mid-thought. A loop back to the opening line works well on short-video platforms.
- Prefer moments where the subject stays near the centre of the frame, since portrait reframing crops the sides.
- Titles and thumbnails for each cut promise exactly what the cut delivers.
